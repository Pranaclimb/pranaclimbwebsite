03 · Activity

Session & Acknowledgement of Risk

Climbing and associated outdoor activity may include risks such as:
  • falls and impact with the climbing surface or ground;
  • falling rock, objects or equipment;
  • uneven, steep, loose, wet or slippery terrain;
  • weather and environmental conditions;
  • equipment misuse, human error and the actions of other people;
  • physical exertion, fatigue and injury.

Pranaclimb will take reasonable steps to manage foreseeable risks, but the inherent risks of participation cannot be completely removed.

04 · Pranaclimb

Breathing, Recording & Media Consent

Pranaclimb coaching may involve observation of breathing during climbing and breathing exercises. Recording permissions are separated so you can choose what you are comfortable with.

04B · Under 18

Parent / Legal Guardian Declaration

This section is required when a participant is under 18.

I confirm that I am the parent or legally appointed guardian of the young person named on this form and have authority to consent to their participation.

I consent to their participation in the Pranaclimb activity described above and acknowledge that climbing and associated outdoor activities involve inherent risks which cannot be completely eliminated.

Where the session is being provided in reliance on the parental exemption from Adventure Activities Licensing, I understand that I must accompany the young person during the activity and remain sufficiently close to take part or provide direct parental supervision. I understand that another responsible adult does not qualify merely because they are a friend or relative.
