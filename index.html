The Pranaclimb Way

Methodology

A breath-led climbing performance system. Field-based, non-invasive, and built for climbing's real-world demands: dynamic movement, anaerobic bursts, expressive breathing, and micro-rests.

BR

Breathing Rate

A real-time effort marker that tracks intensity as you climb, breath by breath.

RPE

CR-10 Scale

Perceived load, tightly correlated with breathing rate and used to identify your thresholds.

HRR

Recovery Speed

Heart-rate recovery at 60 seconds, one of the clearest signals of how quickly you bounce back.

Self-Guided Diagnostic & Training Tool

Remote Breathing Assessment

Your journey begins with a comprehensive remote assessment on and off the rock. The Pranaclimb RBA uses breathing rate, RPE, and heart-rate recovery to identify performance zones, recovery capacity, and breathing efficiency.

  • Breathing Rate and RPE during real climbing movement
  • Expressive breathing, holds, grunts, and high-effort patterns
  • HRR60 recovery tracking between burns
  • Optional deep dive into CO2 tolerance, PEFR, SpO2, and rib mobility

How the Assessment Works

A three-stage process that maps your breathing physiology to real climbing performance in the real world, not a lab.

01

Assess

Capture breathing rate, RPE, expressive breathing, recovery, and optional physiology markers.

02

Interpret

Map effort thresholds, recovery speed, and W'bal depletion so the data means something useful.

03

Identify

Find where performance breaks down: early breath spikes, holds, slow recovery, or stress-led chaos.
