Annie Anderson

Founder of the Pranaclimb Methodology

Independent Researcher · Breathwork Specialist · Cornwall

Annie Anderson is an independent researcher, breathwork specialist and lifelong climber with more than 30 years of worldwide climbing experience. She created the Pranaclimb Methodology to bring breathing physiology, climbing experience and practical coaching together in a field-based system for climbers.

Based on the sea cliffs of Cornwall. Her philosophy — live, love life every day — carried her through three years of Stage 4 Renal Cell Carcinoma and surgery.

Pranaclimb Ltd

Annie Anderson is a breathwork specialist, independent researcher, and lifelong climber. Pranaclimb weaves together more than 30 years of worldwide climbing experience, 25+ years of yoga, meditation, and breathwork, and a decade of independent research into breathing, anatomy & physiology, psychology, neurobiology, and performance science.

Annie is a qualified SPA/RCI Rock Climbing Instructor (1998) and Registered Yoga Teacher RYT 200 (2012).
